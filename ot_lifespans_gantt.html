Old Testament lifespans

How to read this

One bar per person, from birth to death. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, and tap a bar for details. Hover or tap empty space to drop a year line that lists everyone alive in that year.
Lifespan is in the text but its position is estimated
Death age not given, so the bar shows the minimum known age

Perspective

    Method

    Years are Anno Mundi (AM, years from creation), Masoretic text. Adam to Terah are chained from the begetting ages in Gen 5 and Gen 11. Shem's son Arphaxad was born 2 years after the flood (Gen 11:10). Isaac was born when Abraham was 100 and Jacob when Isaac was 60. Joseph's birth comes from Gen 41:46, 45:6 and 47:9.

    Exodus = Abram's call (age 75) + 430 years (Gal 3:17, the "short sojourn" reading of Exod 12:40 used by Ussher). Solomon's 4th year = the 480th year after the Exodus (1 Kgs 6:1). The kings of Judah are then summed reign by reign (accession age + years reigned, Masoretic text) with no co-regencies.

    Approximate BC axis: the fall of Jerusalem in Zedekiah's 11th year is pinned at 586 BC, and everything else is counted back from it. Summing reigns without co-regencies stretches the divided monarchy by about 40 to 50 years compared with modern chronologies like Thiele's, so treat BC years before about 900 BC as approximate.

    Data quality: "explicit" means the text gives the age at death (the verse is quoted). "Derived" means it comes from two textual numbers, such as age at accession plus reign length. Hatched bars mark people whose age is explicit but whose birth year is estimated, and the tooltip explains how. All 107 number phrases were checked word for word against the KJV text in the Theographic dataset.

    Other chronologies differ a lot. The Septuagint adds about 100 years to most begetting ages in Gen 5 and 11 and inserts a second Cainan (Luke 3:36), which puts creation around 5500 BC. The Samaritan Pentateuch shortens Gen 5 and lengthens Gen 11. Only the Masoretic text is shown here.
