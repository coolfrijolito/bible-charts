Bible genealogy charts

Two interactive charts. Pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and tap anything for details. Both work offline.

Old Testament lifespansA timeline of 53 people with ages given in the text, from Adam to Zedekiah, placed in Anno Mundi or approximate BC years with verse references. Tap empty space to see who was alive in that year. Bible family networkA family circle of 2,049 people with God in the centre, Adam and Eve on the first ring and one ring per generation, with a gold main line out to Jesus (switch to a top-down tree any time). Search anyone, explore their family, or find how two people are related. Data: Theographic Bible Metadata (CC BY-SA 4.0), KJV text.
